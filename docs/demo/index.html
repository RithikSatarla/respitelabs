After any crash or change: check, fix and certify the cell in minutes, without a truck roll.

Monitoring tools tell you something is off. Respite Labs tells you what changed and by how much, writes the fix to the controller, proves it worked and signs off the restart.

How it works

Sensors cross-check

Camera, joint encoders and controller settings should agree. We track how far apart they are, in multiples of normal sensor noise.

A 6 second check motion

The arm moves through a short fixed path. That is enough to measure camera pose, fingertip position, joint zeros and command timing.

A fixed marker on the table

It never moves. If the camera sees it shifted, the camera moved. If it looks right but the arm disagrees, a joint drifted.

Geometry and statistics

The checks are geometry and least squares, so the same data gives the same answer and every number can be traced. On real camera images, a small vision model only finds the gripper in each image.
